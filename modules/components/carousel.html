How do I use it?

Temel carousel

Bootstrap carousel davranışı kullanılır; bb-carousel ile tema stili gelir. Nokta stili için bb-carousel-dots veya bb-carousel-bullets.

HTML 
<div id="carouselDots"
     class="carousel slide bb-carousel bb-carousel-dots"
     data-bs-ride="carousel"
     data-bs-touch="true">

    <div class="carousel-inner bb-rounded-lg bb-overflow-hidden bb-demo-slide">
        <div class="carousel-item active">
            <img src="https://picsum.photos/1400/700?1" alt="Slide 1">
        </div>
        <div class="carousel-item">
            <img src="https://picsum.photos/1400/700?2" alt="Slide 2">
        </div>
        <div class="carousel-item">
            <img src="https://picsum.photos/1400/700?7" alt="Slide 3">
        </div>
    </div>

    <div class="carousel-indicators">
        <button type="button" data-bs-target="#carouselDots" data-bs-slide-to="0" class="active" aria-current="true" aria-label="Slide 1"></button>
        <button type="button" data-bs-target="#carouselDots" data-bs-slide-to="1" aria-label="Slide 2"></button>
        <button type="button" data-bs-target="#carouselDots" data-bs-slide-to="2" aria-label="Slide 3"></button>
    </div>
</div>
