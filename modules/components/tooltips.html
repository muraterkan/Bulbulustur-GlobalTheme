How do I use it?

Tooltip

Tetikleyicide data-bs-toggle="tooltip", data-bs-placement ve title. Tema için data-bs-custom-class="bb-tooltip".

HTML 
<button type="button" class="btn btn-primary"
        data-bs-toggle="tooltip"
        data-bs-custom-class="bb-tooltip"
        data-bs-placement="top"
        title="Tooltip on top">
    Tooltip on top
</button>
