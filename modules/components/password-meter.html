How do I use it?

Parola gücü

Kök öğe data-bb-password-meter alır; alan ve gösterge bölümleri bb-password-meter__* sınıflarıyla yazılır. assets/js/core/bb-password-meter.js gerekir.

HTML 
<div class="bb-password-meter" data-bb-password-meter="true" id="bb_password_meter_1">
    <label class="form-label fw-semibold bb-password-meter__label" for="new_password">
        New Password
    </label>

    <div class="bb-password-meter__input-wrap mb-3">
        <input id="new_password"
               class="form-control form-control-lg bb-password-meter__input"
               type="password"
               name="new_password"
               autocomplete="off"
               placeholder="Type a strong password..." />

        <!-- Visibility toggle -->
        <button type="button"
                class="bb-password-meter__toggle"
                data-bb-password-meter-control="visibility"
                aria-label="Passwordyi göster"
                aria-pressed="false">
            <span class="material-symbols-outlined" data-bb-icon="off">visibility_off</span>
            <span class="material-symbols-outlined d-none" data-bb-icon="on">visibility</span>
        </button>
    </div>

    <!-- Highlight meter -->
    <div class="bb-password-meter__meter mb-2"
         data-bb-password-meter-control="highlight"
         role="progressbar"
         aria-valuemin="0"
         aria-valuemax="100"
         aria-valuenow="0">
        <div class="bb-password-meter__bar"></div>
        <div class="bb-password-meter__bar"></div>
        <!-- ... aynı yapıda diğer öğeler ... -->
<!-- ... -->
